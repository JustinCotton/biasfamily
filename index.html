For the first time in nearly 20 years, we're excited to welcome the Bias Family Reunion back to Atlanta, The City of Trees!

After shuttling and ridesharing from the world's businest airport to our host hotel, join us for a 5.30pm happy hour before our meet-and-greet and opening night banquet! We'll reconnect and reintroduce ourselves to our growing family, and map out our weekend together!

Enjoy a complimentary buffet breakfast in the lobby, and then hop on for a tour of Atlanta's iconic Peachtree Street as we shuttle from Downtown to Midtown for our picnic at Piedmont Park! We'll enjoy a low country buffet, card games, and exploring in Atlanta's signature park!

We'll spend the day sightseeing and enjoying all of Atlanta's attractions in small groups, and then relax at the hotel before our banquet, where we'll highlight our talents, honor our ancestors and those we have lost, celebrate the wonders of family, and dance the night away!

   

Our host hotel is Embassy Suites, Centennial Olympic Park, which provides excellent and walkable access to restaurants and attractions, along with a breakfast buffet, daily happy hour, rooftop pool with skyline views, and a Ruth's Chris Steakhouse and Starbucks coffee in the lobby!
